# Ranking Rubric

Use this rubric to keep cross-platform recommendations consistent.

## Default weights

- Price competitiveness: 0.35
- Review and reputation quality: 0.25
- Listing completeness and clarity: 0.15
- Platform and seller protection: 0.15
- Risk adjustment: 0.10

Adjust weights when the user explicitly cares more about safety, warranty, or lowest price.

## Scoring dimensions

### Price competitiveness

Reward:

- clearly below-peer landed price
- included accessories or warranty that improve effective value
- transparent coupon or subsidy pricing

Penalize:

- hidden shipping cost
- missing total price
- price unusually low without supporting trust signals

### Review and reputation quality

Reward:

- large enough review base
- balanced recent review signals
- photo reviews or detailed buyer feedback
- seller history consistent with category

Penalize:

- tiny sample size with suspiciously perfect score
- repeated complaints about authenticity, packaging, defects, or after-sales
- reputation signals that are missing on a high-risk listing

### Listing completeness and clarity

Reward:

- detailed specs
- real product photos
- condition disclosure
- warranty and return details
- accessory list

Penalize:

- vague condition
- incomplete configuration
- no defect disclosure for used items
- unclear seller promise

### Platform and seller protection

Reward:

- official stores
- platform inspection
- clear return window
- invoice or warranty

Penalize:

- off-platform transaction pressure
- no buyer protection details
- weak seller profile for expensive products

### Risk adjustment

Apply explicit deductions for:

- suspiciously low price
- inconsistent specs
- counterfeit complaints
- repair history hidden or unclear
- common scam patterns

## Tie-break rules

When total scores are close:

1. Prefer stronger after-sales support for new products.
2. Prefer lower scam risk for used products.
3. Prefer clearer evidence over nominally lower price.
4. Prefer better landed price only when the trust gap is small.
