# Platform Playbook

Use this reference when gathering listings from Chinese shopping platforms.

## Common search strategy

1. Start with the exact product name.
2. Add key constraints: storage, color, model year, region version, condition.
3. Search the platform directly first.
4. If the platform page is hard to parse, use search with `site:` filters and then open the most relevant result.
5. Drop obvious ad spam and duplicate listings quickly.

## JD

Best for:

- official flagship stores
- strong after-sales policies
- new products with invoice and warranty

Look for:

- `京东自营`
- brand flagship stores
- warranty duration
- visible coupon and subsidy details
- recent review distribution, not only average rating

Common risk cues:

- third-party seller with sparse reviews
- abnormal price gap versus self-operated listings
- hidden shipping cost or slow delivery promises

## Taobao

Best for:

- broad catalog
- accessories and niche SKUs
- alternative sellers when JD is expensive

Look for:

- store age and service score
- buyer review photos
- precise configuration in title and detail page
- return or replacement policy

Common risk cues:

- title keyword stuffing
- inconsistent specs across title, image, and description
- low-detail product pages with aggressive low pricing

## Pinduoduo

Best for:

- low price benchmarking
- subsidy-driven deals
- commodity products with heavy price competition

Look for:

- `百亿补贴`
- final landed price after coupons
- review volume with photo evidence
- seller complaint trends

Common risk cues:

- extremely low pricing without brand or subsidy evidence
- poor packaging or authenticity complaints
- weak return or support terms

## Xianyu

Best for:

- used electronics
- local pickup
- collectors or discontinued items

Look for:

- seller history and reputation
- defect disclosure
- real device photos
- battery health, serial checks, invoice, box, accessories
- willingness to support platform-backed inspection

Common risk cues:

- stock-photo-only listings
- unclear ownership story
- refusal to provide serial or verification details
- pressure to move off-platform

## Zhuanzhuan

Best for:

- used devices with inspection options
- buyers who want more protection than peer-to-peer trading

Look for:

- official inspection or quality report
- return window
- battery or wear grading
- repair history disclosure

Common risk cues:

- inspection report missing from a premium-priced listing
- weak detail on repaired or replaced parts
- price too close to new-market offers

## Comparison heuristics

- Compare only truly similar configurations.
- Separate new, used, refurbished, and unknown-condition items.
- Consider landed price, not sticker price alone.
- Treat strong after-sales support as part of price justification.
- For used goods, accept a higher price if inspection quality materially lowers risk.
